# The pipeline run on every model year

Run on 2026-11-16 on every document that could be obtained for model
years 2020 to 2025. The reference was not read, and the diff has not been
run: only the owner can run it.

## Result

1,049 styles, each from a row of a Toyota pricing document.

| Model year | Styles | MSRP | Delivery fee | Horsepower | Torque | City | Highway | Combined |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 193 | 193 | 190 | 102 | 102 | 175 | 176 | 177 |
| 2021 | 182 | 182 | 182 | 170 | 168 | 176 | 177 | 178 |
| 2022 | 221 | 221 | 215 | 129 | 117 | 205 | 199 | 206 |
| 2023 | 212 | 212 | 208 | 129 | 127 | 198 | 193 | 200 |
| 2024 | 221 | 221 | 221 | 86 | 82 | 196 | 178 | 196 |
| 2025 | 0 | | | | | | | |

The pricing documents also list 20 rows of model years 2018 and 2019,
which are outside the slice.

6,526 claims were admitted and 16 rejected. A cell is empty for one of
two reasons, and the `coverage` view counts them apart:

- **No document states it.** This is nearly all of the empty horsepower
  and torque cells: the line's Product Information document could not be
  obtained.
- **The sources disagree and nothing settles it.** 9 horsepower, 17
  torque, 64 city, 91 highway and 57 combined cells are left open.

## What is held

- 9 pricing documents: two editions for 2019-2020, three for 2020-2021,
  one for 2021-2022, two for 2022-2023 and one for 2023-2024.
- 107 Product Information documents, of which 82 are read. The other 25
  are for model year 2025, which has no pricing document and so no
  styles for a figure to attach to.
- The EPA's `vehicles.csv`.

## What is missing

`just status --missing` lists 48 documents with the file name to save
each under. In order of what they would add:

1. **A 2024-2025 pricing document.** None of its three addresses has a
   capture in the archive. Without it there are no styles for 2025.
2. **Product Information for the lines with no figures.** The largest
   are the 2024 Tundra (43 styles), the 2020, 2022 and 2023 Tacoma (33
   or 34 each), the 2022 and 2024 Highlander (20 and 22), the 2020
   Sienna (18), the 2024 Camry (18) and the 2022 Corolla (15).
3. **Later editions of the pricing documents** for 2022-2023 and
   2023-2024, which would add price history.

## What the run changed in the code

Each was found on a real document and has a test.

- **Snapshots are keyed on the address.** Toyota links one PDF at two
  addresses, which a key on the hash refused.
- **A capture that was cut short** stopped the crawl. It is now recorded
  as unavailable.
- **A row printed twice,** identical in every cell, was rejected as two
  rows with one label.
- **A unit printed as two words** (`lb. ft.`) was not recognised.
- **A header in the second row** of a table whose first row is a title
  was not found.
- **A header cell drawn across several columns** (`i-FORCE` over three
  columns of the 2024 Tacoma's figures) headed only the middle one. The
  other two figures were rejected, and the styles they belong to took
  the middle column's figure. This was the one case found of a wrong
  value, not an empty one.
- **A column header that lists grades** (`L, LE, XLE`) and **powertrain
  words** (`Gas` beside `Hybrid`) are now read as part of a figure's
  scope. They settled 57 torque and 17 horsepower cells.
- **`AWD-e`, `AWD-i`, `CVTi-S`, `A/T` and `M/T`** were not classified.
- **A price list that marks only its manuals** left every other row's
  transmission unknown.
- **`5.7L-V81`,** a footnote mark run into `V8`, was read as 81
  cylinders, which kept the 2020 and 2021 Tundra's figures from every
  style.

## What stays open, and why

- **The EPA file lists two configurations and the price list does not
  say which a grade has.** A 2020 RAV4 AWD is listed with and without
  stop-start, and a 2023 Corolla Hybrid with and without a sport mode.
  Most open fuel economy cells are of this kind.
- **A price row that prints no drive or no engine.** A 2020 Sienna row
  does not say whether it is all-wheel drive, and a Tacoma
  `SR Double Cab 6AT` does not say which engine it has.
- **Electric vehicles.** The bZ4X document gives one motor's torque per
  axle and no total, so the all-wheel-drive torque is open.

## Known gaps

- **2021 and 2022 Sienna engine torque** is rejected. A boxed page
  header in those PDFs is drawn over the table, and `Torque` labels two
  rows of it.
- **Plug-in hybrids' MPGe** is not read. The documents print one MPGe
  figure without saying whether it is city, highway or combined.
- **A range of grades** (`LE → PLTM`) is not expanded.
- The 2023 development year's figures were looked at row by row. The
  other years were not, beyond the checks and the cases above.
